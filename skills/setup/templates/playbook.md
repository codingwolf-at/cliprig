# [PAGE NAME] playbook

## 1. Identity
- What we post: the single best moment from [SOURCE TYPE], cut to 15-45 seconds, with captions.
- Voice: plain, curious, no hype. The creator is the star; we pick and frame the moment.
- Never pose as the creator, the show, the brand or the network. No "official" wording. Never invent quotes, facts or context.

Skip: [NICHE-SPECIFIC SKIPS], finance and trading advice, crypto, gambling, medical claims, politics, anything mocking a private person.

## 3. Posting mix (start here, adjust from data)
- Per platform ([PLATFORMS]): 1-2 a day.
- The same clip may go to each platform once. Never re-upload the same clip to the same account.
- Quality beats volume. Skip a slot rather than post a weak clip.
- Times ([TIME ZONE]): test two windows against each other, for example the target audience's evening vs your evening. Log every post time.

## 4. Sources and copyright
- Clip only with permission: joined campaigns, campaign-supplied footage, or creators who allow clipping in writing. Save the link to the proof in `media/raw/<source>/SOURCE.md`.
- Small creators often say yes to free, credited clips. Ask in writing and save the reply.
- Creative Commons: only plain "CC BY" allows edits and commercial use. ND or NC licenses don't fit.
- "Fair use" and "transformative" edits don't stop automated claims. Don't rely on them.
- No audible music, even under speech. Official-sound campaigns are fine.
- If permission can't be confirmed, don't cut it.

## 5. Making a clip
1. Find moments with `moment-finder`. Pick moments that work without context.
2. Start on the moment itself, not the lead-in. The first 2 seconds decide the view.
3. Hook text on screen, 3-9 words, sentence case. It must be true and delivered by the clip.
4. Word-by-word captions, large, high contrast, in the safe area. Many people watch muted. Caption style: bold (`bold`: white caps with the spoken word in yellow; `clean`: white sentence case, the spoken word brightest; `box`: black caps on a white box). Keep one style so the page is recognisable.
5. Frame the subject vertically (crop, blur fill or facecam). Keep the person delivering the moment in frame.
6. End on the payoff.
7. Material edits, not reposts: our own hook, captions, cut and framing on every clip. Platforms demote pages that mostly repost.

## 6. Captions and copy
- YouTube: title = hook + 1-2 hashtags. Description: one line of context, credit line, campaign hashtags.
- Instagram: hook line, one sentence of context, credit line, 3-5 relevant hashtags.
- X: hook line, blank line, credit line. No hashtags unless the campaign requires one.
- TikTok: hook line, credit line, 3-5 hashtags.
- Credit line: "Clip from <show or channel> with <creator>." Use each campaign's required tags or links exactly.
- Don't repeat a caption or hook within a week. Check `log.md`.

## 7. Campaign workflow (Content Rewards)
1. One Content Rewards account per person. Link every page's social accounts to it; never open a second account.
2. Read the audience rule after joining, before cutting. Tier-1-only campaigns need proof of US/UK/CA/AU viewers.
3. Only videos posted after joining count. Submit each post link within 30 minutes of posting.
4. Unapproved clips are capped across campaigns, not per campaign. Pending, rejected and deleted clips all count, and old ones drop off after about 30 days. Submit only clips likely to be approved. A post you can't submit still grows the page.
5. Keep at least two campaigns active.
6. Log every approved clip in `log.md` and every post in `scoreboard.csv`.

## 8. New accounts
- Days 1-2: set up the profile fully, follow and engage with the niche, no posts, no bio links.
- Day 3: first post from the phone app. Post the first 10 from the phone.
- After 10-15 posts, check the country split (YouTube Studio, Instagram Insights) before joining tier-1-only campaigns.

## 9. Learning loop
- One test at a time (hook style, length, time slot, caption length). Name it in `scoreboard.csv`.
- No verdict before 24 hours; prefer the 72-hour read.
- Weekly: top 3 and bottom 3 posts, median views by pillar and platform. Write the lesson in `learning.md`.
