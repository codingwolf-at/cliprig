# Code of conduct

Cliprig is built by and for people who make clips. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, niche, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Plenty of people here are new to clipping, to Python or to Claude Code.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Credit others' ideas and clips, the same way Cliprig credits creators.
- Respect people's privacy: don't share anyone's personal details, cookies or account information.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwanted attention of any kind.
- Posting someone's private information without their permission.
- Pushing others to clip without permission, pirate content, or break platform or campaign rules.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the issue, comment or pull request. It goes to GitHub, which acts on breaches of its rules. You can also open an issue asking the maintainer for a private way to talk, without including any details. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens

The maintainer reviews every report and may edit or remove content, lock a conversation, or block someone from the project, temporarily or for good, depending on what happened. This applies everywhere Cliprig is discussed on GitHub.
